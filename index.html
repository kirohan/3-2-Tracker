HELPFUL ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions

Does the Google button download the Excel file?

No. Download Excel downloads the spreadsheet. Track with Google opens Google’s copy flow for the online version.

Will cancelled classes hurt attendance?

No. Cancelled classes are not treated as held classes, so they do not reduce the attendance percentage.

Can I still use the tracker offline?

Yes. Students who prefer offline usage can download and use the Excel version separately.

Is my Google account data stored by the website?

No. The website simply redirects to Google’s own copy screen. It does not store passwords, Gmail addresses, attendance logs, or CT marks.
